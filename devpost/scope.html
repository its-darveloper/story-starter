Sims 4 Story Starter (working title)

A guided, pack-aware story planner that interviews a Sims 4 player and hands them a concrete starting plan, so they go into Create-a-Sim with a story already in hand.

The unique kernel

Structure instead of “AI, give me ideas.” The player picks how they think, sets dials (drama, predictability), and says which packs they own; the output is shaped by all of that, so it isn’t the same generic ideas on repeat.

Long-term kernel: a D&D-style roll. When the player picks “surprise me,” a roll with structure behind it decides the twist.

How it fits together

Who it’s for

A Sims 4 player like you: character-first, loves households, outfits and personalities, but stalls once gameplay starts and drifts toward “marry, have a kid, happily ever after.” First for you; eventually other simmers, including people who don’t know what a “skill” or “plugin” is.

Today: brainstorm in their head or in Create-a-Sim, or ask generic AI, which doesn’t understand packs or mechanics.

The core loop (click through)

What “working” looks like

A one-minute demo: open it, answer “what kind of player are you?”, walk the guided flow, set drama/predictability and your packs, and end on a starting plan.

The “oh, that’s cool” beat: the plan visibly reflects the packs you chose and the dials you set. It isn’t generic.

Inspiration & identity

  • Human-centered and intuitive: no jargon, nothing that makes anyone go “wait, what?”
  • Game-night companion energy, with a D&D “roll and see” feel.
  • Counter-example: TikTok-style AI gameplay generation that repeats itself and doesn’t know Sims.

Why this matters to you

“I wish I didn’t have to leave the Sims screen.” You want Create-a-Sim to become execution of a plan, and you want gameplay that surprises you.

The proof-of-concept boundary

In (now)
  • Guided start-of-story flow that branches: character-driven / plot-driven / just an idea
  • Plain-language explanation of each starting style
  • Drama and predictability dials
  • Player selects which packs they own
  • Output: characters (backgrounds + connections), starting plotline, world/lot types, season, loose goal that doesn’t force an ending
  • Medium (skill, plugin, web page…) decided in the spec
Later
  • Mid-game mode: add context (voice or notes) and get new plotlines when play goes stale, the D&D-style roll
  • Load packs / game data from an uploaded file instead of selecting manually
  • Handling players who don’t want surprises (the dial likely covers this)
Explicitly cut
  • Custom content / mods: more complicated; maybe v2
  • Save-file parsing and relationship/event data: unknown data availability, too heavy for the PoC
