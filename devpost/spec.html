Sims 4 Story Starter (working title)

How we build what the PRD describes: a phone-friendly page, a small server on your laptop, pack notes in our own words, and Claude for the language work.

How it works, in plain language

1 · The page
What you see on your phone. Walks through the steps and shows the cards. Never talks to the AI directly.
2 · The little server
Runs on your laptop. Serves the page, holds the secret key, does the behind-the-scenes work.
3 · The pack notes
One file of short, story-focused entries per pack, in our own words, reviewed by you.
4 · The AI (Claude)
Reflects back what you said, asks follow-ups, and writes the plan.

Why plans aren’t generic: before the AI writes anything, the code randomly draws a few story ingredients from your selected packs’ notes, nudged by your sliders. The randomness comes from the code, not the AI’s habits.

Look and feel, in build terms

  • Palette: hot magenta/pink base, baby blue and lavender, aqua accents, white margin.
  • Shapes: rounded pill buttons with colored outlines, a vertical stack of icon tiles, sticker-like card tiles.
  • Type: rounded sans for UI, italic script headings, with offline-safe fallbacks.
  • Details: pixel-square corners, speech bubbles for follow-ups, sparkles, “+” marks.
  • Imagery: interface decoration only (CSS and inline SVG), all original.
  • Readability wins: decoration never sits behind long text.

Where it runs and how someone tries it

Start: node --env-file=.env server/server.js, then open the address it prints on your phone (same Wi-Fi), or http://localhost:3000 on the laptop. Record the demo from the phone or laptop browser. A short demo video and a public GitHub repo are required; a public link is later.

What could break, and what you’d see

The AI is slow or fails

A friendly loading state with retry; your inputs are kept.

The AI returns the wrong structure

The server retries once; then a plain “try again” message.

No pack chosen, or a pack has no notes yet

The draw uses general notes; a plan is still produced.

Phone can’t reach the laptop

README checklist (same Wi-Fi, right address, laptop awake); the demo can use the laptop browser.

Laptop address changes

Plans saved at one address don’t appear at another. Known limit until there’s a stable public link.

What we simplified, and why

  • No framework, no packages · nothing to install, fewer unfamiliar errors
  • Browser storage, no accounts · matches “same device”
  • Four packs’ notes first · each needs your review
  • Decoration only, no illustrated characters · Y2K feel at the least asset cost
  • Laptop server, no hosting · free and simple; public link comes later
  • Start-of-story draw, not the full mid-game roll · proves the anti-generic idea first

Decisions and open issues

  • Plain HTML/CSS/JS, Node with no packages, Claude · recommended, you accepted
  • Pack notes drafted from sources, reviewed by you, in our own words · you agreed

  • Random ingredient draw before the AI writes · recommended, you accepted

Open issues
