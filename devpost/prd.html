Sims 4 Story Starter (working title)

Scope said “a guided flow, dials, packs, and a starting plan.” Now we know exactly what each of those does.

Guiding principle

Human-centered. Low cognitive load. Plain language. Nothing that makes anyone say “wait, what?”

The core journey

Explore each step

Try the pack picker (sample behavior)

Pick as many as you own. Or skip. Either way you move forward. Notice the supernatural checkbox below.

Look and feel

  • Vibe: My Scene / Polly Pocket, Y2K web: glossy, bubbly, colorful, nostalgic.
  • Metaphor: a little world you peek into; cards you open; tarot / Yu-Gi-Oh “pull from a deck” as a possible fit.
  • Priority: looks and feels good, but low cognitive load beats decoration.
  • Not yet set: exact colors, fonts, and how far to take the deck metaphor.

Boundaries

Building now
  • Welcome · starting-style choice · yap box with nudges · reflect and follow-up · four sliders (drama, predictability, relationship planning, pace) with live descriptions + supernatural opt-in · pack picking with skip · plan as a stack of cards led by the chosen style · save on this device and reopen · phone-friendly layout
Deferred from the POC
  • Pack-list file upload: your pack folder names identify packs, so it looks feasible, but clicking is enough for now
  • Mid-game “what happens next” and the roll
  • Reading save files / raw game-data extraction
  • Cross-device sync and sharing plans
  • Playing inside the game (a mod in Create-a-Sim)
Non-goals
  • Not a replacement for Create-a-Sim · no custom content / mods · no accounts · never forces a story ending
Open questions
  • Resolved: used beside the game (second screen / phone), saved on the same device; dial controls; skipped packs and the supernatural checkbox
  • Skill vs. app vs. other medium — must be decided in the spec
  • How is pack content sourced? — descriptive info in our own words; exact approach in the spec
  • Resolved: supernatural-capable packs are Werewolves, Realm of Magic, Vampires, Paranormal Stuff, and Get to Work (aliens)
  • What starting styles are missing? — research; can happen during the build
  • How do the PoC outputs prove they aren’t generic? — candidate: sliders and packs visibly change the plan
